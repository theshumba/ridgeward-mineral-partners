Our approach

Start with the project.

This is the approach we intend to use when considering a partnership.

  1. 01

    Understand

    Establish the project’s stage, the parties involved and what is being proposed.

  2. 02

    Assess

    Examine the rights, operating evidence, costs and material risks with the appropriate specialists.

  3. 03

    Structure

    Define ownership, contributions, responsibilities and commercial terms for the proposed arrangement.

  4. 04

    Report

    Agree how project activity, expenditure and outcomes would be recorded and communicated.

What matters

Clear terms
from the outset.

Ownership

Be clear about what each party would own and the rights attached to it.

Capital use

Explain what funding would pay for and the assumptions behind the budget.

Responsibilities

Identify who would operate the project and who would be accountable for each part.

Reporting

Agree what information partners would receive and how often.

Questions

Before the conversation.

What is Ridgeward Mineral Partners?+

A mining investment and development company being established with an initial focus on Zimbabwe. The aim is to bring investors, mine owners and qualified operators together around defined projects.

Can I invest through this website?+

No investments or payments are accepted through this website. It provides an introduction to Ridgeward and a way to prepare an enquiry. Any future participation would depend on the project, documentation and applicable requirements.

What kinds of participation are you considering?+

The proposed business may consider project ownership, equipment participation and operating partnerships. The suitable arrangement would depend on the project and the parties involved. These are possible approaches, not current offers.

How are returns determined?+

There is no promised return on this website. Any entitlement would depend on the specific commercial arrangement and its terms. Mining projects can experience losses, delays and operating difficulties.

What principles guide the arrangements?+

The intention is to build around genuine ownership, clearly agreed responsibilities and appropriate commercial contracts, without interest-bearing lending. Each proposed arrangement would need review against those principles. This website does not claim religious certification.

What should I include in an enquiry?+

Tell us your role, the project or area of interest, and the conversation you would like to have. At this stage, do not include identity documents, bank details or confidential project files.
